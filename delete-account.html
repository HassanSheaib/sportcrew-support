Delete Account

Information about deleting your SportCrew account and associated data

Delete Your Account From Inside the App

SportCrew users can permanently delete their account directly from inside the application.

To delete your account:

  • Open the SportCrew app
  • Go to Settings
  • Open the Delete Account section
  • Follow the account deletion workflow

Once the deletion process is completed, your account and associated data are permanently deleted and cannot be restored.

Alternative Deletion Request Method

If you are unable to access your account or the application, you may also contact us directly to request account deletion.

Email: hhs.softwaredev@gmail.com

Please include the email address or phone number associated with your SportCrew account so we can verify ownership before processing the request.

Data Deletion Policy

When an account deletion request is completed, all associated personal data related to the account is permanently deleted from our systems.

Deleted data cannot be recovered or restored.
