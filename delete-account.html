Delete Your Sport Crew Account

Sport Crew users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Sport Crew account, please contact us at:

support@sport-crew.com

Please include the email address or phone number associated with your account. We may contact you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • User profile information
  • Account credentials
  • Game participation data
  • Notifications
  • Discussion and chat data where applicable

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained temporarily for legal, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, and operational purposes.

Retained data may be stored for up to 30 days unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Deletion Processing Time

Account deletion requests are typically processed within 30 days after ownership verification is completed.
